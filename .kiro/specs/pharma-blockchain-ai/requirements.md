# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Pharma Blockchain AI system is a drug authentication and anti-counterfeit platform for pharmaceutical supply chains. It tracks every drug unit from manufacturer to end consumer using a dual-blockchain architecture: Polygon Amoy testnet for public, tamper-proof audit records, and a simplified Hyperledger Fabric private network for sensitive business data. An AI risk scoring engine continuously evaluates each drug's authenticity signals and writes scores on-chain. QR codes allow anyone — including consumers and regulators — to instantly verify a drug's authenticity, full chain-of-custody, GPS journey, and risk status by scanning a printed label.

The system extends an existing working prototype that already includes: a Solidity smart contract (Drug struct, roles, createDrug, transferDrug, getDrug, updateRiskScore), a Node.js/Express backend (basic CRUD, QR generation, HTML verification page), and a React frontend (create drug form, get drug, QR generation/scanning, dark mode toggle, basic dashboard stats).

## Glossary

- **System**: The Pharma Blockchain AI platform as a whole.
- **Smart_Contract**: The `PharmaSupplyChain.sol` Solidity contract deployed on Polygon Amoy testnet.
- **Backend**: The Node.js/Express API server that bridges the frontend, Smart_Contract, AI_Engine, and Hyperledger_Network.
- **Frontend**: The React single-page application used by all roles.
- **AI_Engine**: The Python Flask (or Node.js) microservice that computes risk scores.
- **Hyperledger_Network**: The local simplified Hyperledger Fabric private blockchain network.
- **Admin**: A privileged wallet address that can assign roles and update risk scores on-chain.
- **Manufacturer**: A supply chain actor authorized to register new drugs on-chain.
- **Distributor**: A supply chain actor authorized to receive and forward drugs.
- **Pharmacy**: A supply chain actor authorized to receive drugs and dispense them to consumers.
- **Consumer**: An end user or member of the public who can verify a drug via QR scan (read-only, no authentication required).
- **Drug**: A pharmaceutical product unit identified by a unique `drugID`, represented as a struct on-chain.
- **DrugID**: A globally unique string identifier for a Drug, chosen by the Manufacturer at registration time.
- **BatchNumber**: A string identifying the manufacturing batch a Drug belongs to.
- **ExpiryTimestamp**: A Unix epoch integer (seconds) representing the date after which a Drug is considered expired.
- **TransferEvent**: A record of a single custody handoff, containing: drugID, from address, to address, GPS coordinates, and a Unix timestamp.
- **TransferHistory**: The append-only ordered list of all TransferEvents for a given Drug, stored on-chain.
- **RiskScore**: An integer between 0 and 100 inclusive representing the AI-assessed counterfeit risk of a Drug (0 = lowest risk, 100 = highest risk).
- **RiskCategory**: A human-readable classification derived from RiskScore — uncolored (score = 0), Low (1–24), Medium (25–49), High (50–74), Critical (75–100).
- **DrugStatus**: An enumeration: `Active`, `Recalled`, or `Expired`. A Drug is `Expired` when the current block timestamp exceeds its ExpiryTimestamp.
- **QRCode**: A 2-D barcode image encoding a VerifyURL, generated by the Backend and associated with a Drug.
- **VerifyURL**: A public URL of the form `{BASE_URL}/verify/{drugID}` that opens the public verification page for a Drug.
- **GPS_Coordinate**: A pair of decimal numbers: latitude in [-90, 90] and longitude in [-180, 180].
- **JWT**: JSON Web Token used for stateless authentication of supply chain actors (Admin, Manufacturer, Distributor, Pharmacy).
- **Private_Collection**: A Hyperledger Fabric side-database holding sensitive business data (pricing, internal batch records) visible only to authorized organizations.
- **Sync_Event**: A Hyperledger chaincode action that mirrors a key drug lifecycle event to the Smart_Contract on Polygon for public verifiability.
- **WebSocket**: A persistent bidirectional connection used to push real-time transfer updates to the Frontend.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Drug Registration

**User Story:** As a Manufacturer, I want to register a new drug on the blockchain, so that it has a tamper-proof identity from the moment it is produced.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Manufacturer submits a registration request with a unique DrugID, name, BatchNumber, ExpiryTimestamp, and manufacturer wallet address, THE Smart_Contract SHALL create a Drug record on-chain and emit a `DrugCreated` event.
2. IF a Manufacturer submits a registration request with a DrugID that already exists on-chain, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction with an error indicating the Drug already exists.
3. IF a wallet without the Manufacturer role calls the drug registration function, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction with an unauthorized role error.
4. WHEN a Drug is created, THE Smart_Contract SHALL set its initial DrugStatus to `Active`, its initial RiskScore to 0, and its currentOwner to the Manufacturer's wallet address.
5. WHEN a Drug is successfully registered, THE Backend SHALL store the manufacturer's name and contact details as off-chain metadata in the Database linked to the DrugID.
6. THE Smart_Contract SHALL require that ExpiryTimestamp is a positive integer greater than the current block timestamp at the time of registration, ensuring the expiry date represents a future date.

---

### Requirement 2: Role-Based Access Control

**User Story:** As an Admin, I want to assign roles to wallet addresses, so that only authorized actors can perform their designated supply chain functions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Smart_Contract SHALL support exactly five roles: Admin, Manufacturer, Distributor, Pharmacy, and Consumer (no-auth public).
2. WHEN the Admin calls `assignRole` with a valid wallet address and a valid role enum value, THE Smart_Contract SHALL update the role mapping for that address and emit a `RoleAssigned` event.
3. IF a non-Admin wallet calls `assignRole`, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction with an unauthorized error.
4. WHEN a wallet address has been assigned a role, THE Backend SHALL issue a JWT to that address upon successful login, encoding the role as a claim.
5. WHEN a JWT-protected endpoint is called, THE Backend SHALL validate the JWT signature and role claim before processing the request.
6. IF a JWT is expired or invalid, THEN THE Backend SHALL return an HTTP 401 response.
7. WHERE a Consumer accesses the public verification endpoint (`/verify/:drugID`), THE Backend SHALL process the request without requiring a JWT, but the Consumer role must have been assigned by the Admin to the accessing wallet address; completely anonymous, unregistered addresses may not be assigned the Consumer role.

---

### Requirement 3: Drug Ownership Transfer

**User Story:** As a supply chain actor (Manufacturer, Distributor, or Pharmacy), I want to transfer custody of a drug to the next party and record GPS coordinates, so that the full chain-of-custody is immutably tracked.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the current owner calls `transferDrug` with a valid DrugID, a valid recipient address, and valid GPS_Coordinates, THE Smart_Contract SHALL update the Drug's `currentOwner` to the recipient address and append a TransferEvent to the Drug's TransferHistory.
2. IF a wallet that is not the current owner of a Drug calls `transferDrug`, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction with a "Not owner" error.
3. IF `transferDrug` is called with a DrugID that does not exist, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction with a "Drug not found" error.
4. THE Smart_Contract SHALL store each TransferEvent with: `from` address, `to` address, Unix timestamp of the block, latitude, longitude, and an auto-incrementing sequence index.
5. WHEN a transfer is recorded, THE Smart_Contract SHALL emit a `DrugTransferred` event containing drugID, from, to, latitude, longitude, and timestamp.
6. THE Smart_Contract SHALL preserve the order of TransferEvents such that index 0 is always the first transfer (from Manufacturer) and subsequent indexes are always later in time.
7. IF GPS_Coordinates are outside the valid range (latitude outside [-90, 90] or longitude outside [-180, 180]), THEN THE Backend SHALL reject the transfer request with an HTTP 400 validation error before submitting the transaction on-chain.
8. WHEN a drug is transferred, THE Backend SHALL trigger the AI_Engine to recalculate and update the Drug's RiskScore.

---

### Requirement 4: Transfer History and Timeline

**User Story:** As any authenticated actor or public consumer, I want to view the complete transfer history of a drug, so that I can trace its journey from manufacturer to my hands.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a request is made to the Backend for a Drug's transfer history by DrugID, THE Backend SHALL return the complete ordered TransferHistory array from the Smart_Contract.
2. THE Backend SHALL format each TransferEvent in the response with: sequence index, from address (with role label), to address (with role label), human-readable timestamp, latitude, longitude, and location name (reverse-geocoded where available).
3. THE Smart_Contract SHALL guarantee that the TransferHistory for any Drug is append-only; no existing TransferEvent SHALL be modifiable or deletable after it is written.
4. WHEN a drug's transfer history is returned, THE Backend SHALL include the current DrugStatus, current RiskScore, and RiskCategory in the response.
5. WHERE a drug has zero transfers (just registered), THE Backend SHALL return an empty TransferHistory array and automatically designate the Manufacturer (the address that called `createDrug`) as the current owner.

---

### Requirement 5: Drug Status Management

**User Story:** As an Admin or authorized actor, I want drugs to reflect accurate lifecycle status (Active, Recalled, Expired), so that counterfeit or unsafe drugs are flagged immediately.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a Drug's ExpiryTimestamp is less than the current block timestamp, THE Smart_Contract SHALL return `Expired` as the Drug's status regardless of any other field, including RiskScore.
2. WHEN the Admin calls `recallDrug` with a valid DrugID, THE Smart_Contract SHALL set the Drug's status to `Recalled` and emit a `DrugRecalled` event.
3. IF a non-Admin wallet calls `recallDrug`, THEN THE Smart_Contract SHALL reject the transaction.
4. WHEN a Drug is `Recalled`, THE Backend SHALL include a recall notice in all verification and history responses for that Drug.
5. THE Smart_Contract SHALL expose a `getDrugStatus` view function that returns the current DrugStatus for any existing DrugID, computing `Expired` dynamically from the block timestamp.

---

### Requirement 6: AI Risk Scoring Engine

**User Story:** As the system, I want to automatically compute and update a risk score for each drug based on supply chain signals, so that suspicious drugs are flagged before they reach consumers.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE AI_Engine SHALL accept a risk scoring request containing: ExpiryTimestamp, number of transfers, transfer timestamps, GPS_Coordinates sequence, BatchNumber recall flag, and manufacturer reputation score.
2. WHEN a risk scoring request is received, THE AI_Engine SHALL compute a RiskScore in the range [0, 100] inclusive and a RiskCategory: uncolored when score is 0, Low for scores 1–24, Medium for 25–49, High for 50–74, and Critical for 75–100.
3. THE AI_Engine SHALL apply the following scoring rules as additive components summing to a maximum of 100:
   - Expiry proximity: +20 if expiry is within 30 days; +10 if within 90 days.
   - Transfer count anomaly: +15 if the number of transfers exceeds 10 for a single drug unit.
   - Transfer time anomaly: +20 if any two consecutive transfers are less than 60 seconds apart (timestamp jump).
   - Location jump anomaly: +25 if the geographic distance between two consecutive GPS_Coordinates implies a speed greater than 1000 km/h.
   - Batch recall history: +30 if the BatchNumber is flagged as recalled in the Database.
4. WHEN the AI_Engine returns a RiskScore, THE Backend SHALL call `updateRiskScore` on the Smart_Contract using the Admin wallet to write the score on-chain; IF the blockchain call fails, THE Backend SHALL retry with exponential backoff (initial delay 2s, maximum 3 retries) before logging the failure.
5. IF the AI_Engine service is unavailable, THEN THE Backend SHALL retain the last known RiskScore and log the failure without blocking the transfer transaction.
6. THE AI_Engine SHALL return the RiskScore within 5 seconds of receiving a scoring request under normal operating conditions.
7. WHEN a RiskScore is updated on-chain, THE Smart_Contract SHALL emit a `RiskScoreUpdated` event with the drugID and new score.
8. THE AI_Engine SHALL expose a `/score` HTTP endpoint accepting POST requests with the scoring inputs described in criterion 1.

### Requirement 7: QR Code Generation and Verification

**User Story:** As a Manufacturer, I want to generate a downloadable QR code for each drug I produce, so that anyone in the supply chain or a consumer can scan it to instantly verify the drug's authenticity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Manufacturer requests QR code generation for a DrugID that exists on-chain, THE Backend SHALL generate a QRCode encoding the VerifyURL `{BASE_URL}/verify/{drugID}` and return it as a base64-encoded PNG data URL.
2. THE Backend SHALL ensure that for any registered DrugID, the QRCode encodes a VerifyURL that, when fetched, returns information for exactly that DrugID and no other.
3. WHEN a Consumer or any user fetches the VerifyURL, THE Backend SHALL return a public verification page showing: drug name, BatchNumber, Manufacturer name, ExpiryTimestamp formatted as a human-readable date, current DrugStatus, RiskScore with color-coded RiskCategory, and the full TransferHistory timeline.
4. IF a VerifyURL is fetched for a DrugID that does not exist on-chain, THEN THE Backend SHALL return a page or JSON response clearly indicating the drug is not found and potentially counterfeit.
5. THE Backend SHALL allow the Frontend to render a downloadable/printable QR code image for offline label printing without requiring an internet connection at the point of printing.
6. WHEN a QR code is scanned using the html5-qrcode scanner in the Frontend, THE Frontend SHALL extract the DrugID from the decoded URL and call the drug verification endpoint automatically.

---

### Requirement 8: Live GPS Tracking and Map View

**User Story:** As any supply chain actor or Consumer, I want to see the drug's physical journey plotted on a map with timestamps, so that I can visually confirm the route makes geographic sense.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a transfer is recorded with GPS_Coordinates, THE Backend SHALL persist the latitude, longitude, actor role, and timestamp to the Database as a GPS log entry linked to the DrugID.
2. WHEN a tracking data request is made for a DrugID, THE Backend SHALL return an ordered list of GPS log entries forming the journey path.
3. THE Frontend SHALL render the GPS journey path on a Leaflet.js map, placing a marker at each recorded GPS_Coordinate, labeled with the actor role, timestamp, and sequence number.
4. THE Frontend SHALL draw a polyline connecting the GPS markers in chronological transfer order to show the drug's travel route.
5. WHEN a new transfer occurs while the Frontend is displaying the tracking map for that DrugID, THE Frontend SHALL update the map with the new GPS marker without requiring a full page reload.
6. THE Frontend SHALL display a timeline list alongside the map showing each transfer step as: icon (role), actor name or address, timestamp, and GPS_Coordinate or location name.

---

### Requirement 9: Role-Based Dashboards

**User Story:** As an authenticated supply chain actor, I want a dashboard tailored to my role, so that I can see only the actions and data relevant to my function.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Manufacturer logs in, THE Frontend SHALL display the Manufacturer dashboard showing: a list of drugs registered by this Manufacturer, options to register a new drug, generate a QR code, and view transfer history per drug.
2. WHEN a Distributor logs in, THE Backend SHALL verify the Distributor's JWT before rendering the dashboard; WHEN a valid Distributor JWT is present, THE Frontend SHALL display the Distributor dashboard showing: drugs currently owned by this Distributor, options to record a transfer with GPS_Coordinates to a Pharmacy or another Distributor, and a transfer history view.
3. WHEN a Pharmacy logs in, THE Frontend SHALL display the Pharmacy dashboard showing: drugs currently held by this Pharmacy, an option to mark a drug as dispensed to a Consumer, and a transfer history view.
4. WHEN a Consumer or unauthenticated user accesses the public verification page, THE Frontend SHALL display the Consumer verification view showing: drug info, DrugStatus, RiskScore with color-coded badge, full TransferHistory timeline, and the GPS journey map.
5. THE Frontend SHALL restrict navigation links and action buttons based on JWT role claims stored in Frontend state, such that a Distributor cannot access Manufacturer-only actions and vice versa; IF the restriction mechanism fails for any reason, THE Frontend SHALL fall back to unrestricted navigation rather than blocking the user entirely.
6. THE Frontend SHALL display a global header with: the logged-in user's role and wallet address (truncated), a logout button, and the dark/light mode toggle inherited from the existing codebase.

---

### Requirement 10: JWT Authentication and Session Management

**User Story:** As a supply chain actor, I want to log in with my wallet credentials and receive a session token, so that my identity and role are verified on every API call.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user submits a login request with a valid wallet address and a signed message proving wallet ownership, THE Backend SHALL first verify that the wallet address has an assigned role on-chain; IF no role is assigned, THE Backend SHALL return HTTP 403 immediately without performing signature verification; WHEN a role is found, THE Backend SHALL then verify the signature and, IF signature verification or JWT issuance fails for any reason, SHALL return HTTP 401 and treat the login as failed.
2. THE Backend SHALL set the JWT expiry to 8 hours from issuance.
3. IF a login is attempted for a wallet address that has no role assigned on-chain, THEN THE Backend SHALL return an HTTP 403 response immediately, without performing any signature verification.
4. WHEN a JWT-protected route is accessed with a valid, non-expired JWT, THE Backend SHALL process the request and attach the decoded wallet address and role to the request context.
5. IF a JWT-protected route is accessed with an expired JWT, THEN THE Backend SHALL return an HTTP 401 response with a message directing the user to re-authenticate.
6. THE Backend SHALL never store raw private keys; wallet authentication SHALL use challenge-response signature verification only.

### Requirement 11: Hyperledger Fabric Private Data

**User Story:** As a supply chain partner, I want sensitive business information (pricing, internal batch records) stored privately and accessible only to authorized organizations, while public drug identity data remains on Polygon.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Hyperledger_Network SHALL maintain a Private_Collection accessible only to Manufacturer and Distributor organizations, storing: drug unit cost, internal batch production notes, and supplier invoice reference.
2. THE Backend SHALL allow private fields to be stored in the Private_Collection through any valid Hyperledger chaincode operation, not exclusively manufacturer drug creation, provided the calling organization has write access to the Private_Collection.
3. IF a Pharmacy organization queries the Private_Collection, THEN THE Hyperledger_Network SHALL return an access denied error.
4. WHEN a key drug lifecycle event occurs on Hyperledger (drug creation or transfer), THE Hyperledger chaincode SHALL emit a Sync_Event that THE Backend SHALL relay to the Smart_Contract on Polygon; IF the relay fails, THE Backend SHALL retry with exponential backoff until the sync succeeds, continuing to retry indefinitely until the event is confirmed on Polygon.
5. THE Backend SHALL expose a `/fabric/drug/:id` endpoint (JWT-protected, Manufacturer or Distributor role only) that returns the full Hyperledger record including private fields for authorized callers.
6. THE Hyperledger_Network SHALL be runnable as a local development network using Docker Compose, with setup documented in a README step.

### Requirement 12: Real-Time Transfer Updates via WebSocket

**User Story:** As a Distributor or Pharmacy watching the tracking dashboard, I want to receive live updates when a drug I am tracking is transferred, so that I always see the current state without manual refresh.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Backend SHALL maintain a WebSocket server on the same port as the HTTP API (or a dedicated port configured via environment variable).
2. WHEN a client connects via WebSocket and sends a `subscribe` message with a DrugID, THE Backend SHALL only accept the subscription message if the WebSocket server is active; IF the WebSocket server is not yet active, THE Backend SHALL queue the subscription attempt and process it when the server becomes available.
3. WHEN a transfer transaction for a subscribed DrugID is confirmed on-chain, THE Backend SHALL broadcast a JSON message to all subscribers of that DrugID containing: new owner address, GPS_Coordinates, timestamp, and updated RiskScore.
4. IF a WebSocket client disconnects, THEN THE Backend SHALL remove the client from all subscription lists without affecting other subscribers.
5. WHERE the client browser does not support WebSocket, THE Frontend SHALL fall back to polling the transfer history endpoint every 10 seconds.

### Requirement 13: Drug Recall Management

**User Story:** As an Admin or Regulator, I want to recall a drug batch across the entire supply chain, so that all downstream actors are immediately notified and the drug is marked unsafe.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Admin calls the recall endpoint with a DrugID or BatchNumber, THE Backend SHALL call `recallDrug` on the Smart_Contract, setting the DrugStatus to `Recalled` for all matching drugs.
2. WHEN a Drug is recalled, THE Backend SHALL broadcast a WebSocket notification to all subscribers of that DrugID with a recall alert.
3. WHEN a recalled Drug is verified via the VerifyURL, THE Backend SHALL display a prominent recall warning at the top of the verification page before any other drug information, and SHALL still render the full drug information below the warning.
4. THE Backend SHALL expose a `/admin/recall` endpoint (JWT-protected, Admin role only) accepting a DrugID or BatchNumber for batch recalls.
5. WHEN a Drug is recalled, THE AI_Engine SHALL assign it a RiskScore of 100 and RiskCategory of `Critical` regardless of other signals.

### Requirement 14: Database and Off-Chain Metadata

**User Story:** As the system, I want to store user accounts, GPS logs, cached drug metadata, and AI scoring history in an off-chain database, so that the application is fast and reduces expensive on-chain reads.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Backend SHALL use MongoDB or SQLite as the off-chain Database for: user account records, GPS transfer logs, AI scoring history, and drug metadata cache.
2. WHEN a drug is registered, THE Backend SHALL create a corresponding Database record with the manufacturer's display name, contact email, and registration timestamp.
3. THE Backend SHALL cache the result of `getDrug` calls from the Smart_Contract in the Database with a TTL of exactly 60 seconds; any cache configuration deviating from exactly 60 seconds SHALL be rejected at startup.
4. WHEN a transfer occurs, THE Backend SHALL immediately invalidate the cached entry for the affected DrugID so the next read reflects the updated on-chain state.
5. THE Backend SHALL log every AI scoring request and response (inputs, RiskScore, timestamp, drugID) to the Database for audit purposes.

### Requirement 15: Frontend Dark/Light Mode and Accessibility

**User Story:** As a user of the system, I want a clean, accessible interface with dark and light mode support, so that the application is comfortable to use in any environment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Frontend SHALL preserve the existing dark/light mode toggle and apply the selected theme consistently across all dashboard views, including the new role-based pages, map view, and QR pages.
2. THE Frontend SHALL display RiskScore with color-coded visual indicators: green for Low (1–24), yellow for Medium (25–49), orange for High (50–74), and red for Critical (75–100). WHEN the RiskScore is exactly 0, THE Frontend SHALL display the score without any color-coded badge.
3. THE Frontend SHALL render all interactive elements (buttons, forms, links) with sufficient color contrast ratio (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text per WCAG 2.1 AA) in both dark and light themes.
4. THE Frontend SHALL display a loading spinner or skeleton state during any asynchronous operation (API call, blockchain transaction wait) to indicate progress to the user.
5. WHEN a blockchain transaction fails or returns an error, THE Frontend SHALL display a human-readable error message including the reason string from the smart contract revert.

### Requirement 16: Environment Configuration and Deployment

**User Story:** As a developer or evaluator, I want the system to be fully configurable via environment variables, so that I can run it locally or deploy it without changing source code.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Backend SHALL read all external configuration from environment variables: `RPC_URL`, `PRIVATE_KEY`, `CONTRACT_ADDRESS`, `BASE_URL`, `JWT_SECRET`, `MONGO_URI` (or `SQLITE_PATH`), `AI_ENGINE_URL`, `PORT`.
2. THE Smart_Contract SHALL be deployable to Polygon Amoy testnet using the existing Hardhat configuration by running a single deploy script.
3. THE Backend SHALL start with a single `npm start` command after environment variables are set.
4. THE Frontend SHALL read the backend API base URL from a `REACT_APP_API_URL` environment variable and use it for all Axios requests, replacing the hardcoded render.com URL in the existing codebase.
5. THE Hyperledger_Network SHALL start with a single `docker-compose up` command from the fabric directory.
6. THE AI_Engine SHALL start with a single `python app.py` or `npm start` command and be reachable at the URL configured in `AI_ENGINE_URL`.

## Correctness Properties

The following properties define invariants and round-trip conditions that must hold for all valid system states. These are suitable for property-based testing.

### Property 1: Unregistered Drug Is Not Verifiable

FOR ALL DrugID values that have never been passed to `createDrug` on the Smart_Contract, THE Smart_Contract SHALL revert when `getDrug` is called with that DrugID, and THE Backend `/verify/:drugID` endpoint SHALL return a not-found response (HTTP 404 or an HTML page clearly indicating drug not found).

### Property 2: Only Current Owner Can Transfer

FOR ALL DrugIDs, FOR ALL wallet addresses `w` where `w` is not equal to `drugs[drugID].currentOwner`, THE Smart_Contract SHALL revert when `transferDrug(drugID, anyRecipient)` is called from `w`.

### Property 3: Transfer History Is Immutable and Append-Only

FOR ALL DrugIDs with a TransferHistory of length `n`, after any number of additional `transferDrug` calls, the first `n` entries of the TransferHistory SHALL remain identical in content and order to the original first `n` entries, and the total length SHALL be greater than or equal to `n`.

### Property 4: Risk Score Is Bounded

FOR ALL inputs to the AI_Engine scoring function, THE AI_Engine SHALL return a RiskScore `r` such that `0 <= r <= 100`. FOR ALL `updateRiskScore` calls, THE Smart_Contract SHALL store a value `r` such that `0 <= r <= 100`.

### Property 5: Expired Drug Always Shows Expired Status

FOR ALL DrugIDs where `block.timestamp > drug.expiryDate`, THE Smart_Contract `getDrugStatus` function SHALL return `Expired`, and THE Backend verification response SHALL display `Expired` status, regardless of the value of RiskScore or any other field.

### Property 6: GPS Coordinates Are Valid

FOR ALL transfer requests processed by the Backend, IF latitude `lat` is outside [-90, 90] OR longitude `lng` is outside [-180, 180], THEN THE Backend SHALL reject the request with HTTP 400 and SHALL NOT submit a transaction to the Smart_Contract. FOR ALL GPS log entries stored in the Database, `lat` SHALL satisfy `-90 <= lat <= 90` and `lng` SHALL satisfy `-180 <= lng <= 180`.

### Property 7: QR Code Resolves to Correct Drug

FOR ALL registered DrugIDs `d`, the QRCode generated by the Backend for `d` SHALL encode a URL that, when fetched, returns verification data for DrugID `d` and only DrugID `d`. Formally: `decode(QRCode(d))` yields a URL `u`, and `fetch(u).drugID == d`.

### Property 8: Risk Score of Recalled Drug Is Critical

FOR ALL DrugIDs where DrugStatus is `Recalled`, THE AI_Engine SHALL assign RiskScore = 100 and RiskCategory = `Critical`, and THE Smart_Contract SHALL store RiskScore = 100 for that DrugID after the recall is processed.

### Property 9: Transfer Count Is Consistent

FOR ALL DrugIDs, the length of the on-chain TransferHistory array SHALL equal the total number of successful `transferDrug` transactions ever submitted for that DrugID. This count SHALL never decrease.

### Property 10: JWT Role Claim Matches On-Chain Role

FOR ALL authenticated requests where the JWT contains a role claim `r` for wallet address `w`, THE Smart_Contract `roles[w]` mapping SHALL return a role value consistent with `r`. A JWT claiming Manufacturer role for a wallet that the Smart_Contract records as Distributor SHALL be rejected by the Backend.
